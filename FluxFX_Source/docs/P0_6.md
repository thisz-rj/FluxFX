# P0.6 — interactive smoke controls

The dense pressure-projected smoke prototype now accepts source and flow edits
between GPU steps. Tested September 21, 2026 on Blender 5.3.0 Alpha b2e052b7172a,
Apple M5 Pro, Metal. No shader or numerical discretization changes in this stage.

## Using the controls

Open the FluxFX sidebar, Reset, then Play. The Source section remains editable:

- Move X/Y/Z within the 1 m box or change source radius.
- Toggle **Emit smoke and heat**. Off stops both continuous sources; previously
  emitted smoke and heat continue to advect and decay.
- Adjust density and heat emission rates independently. Negative heat rates cool.

The Flow section exposes live decay, cooling, heat lift, density weight, and
pressure-iteration budget. Edits take effect on the next simulation step. Moving
in Y can move emission away from the displayed slice; adjust **Y slice** to see
it. Source spheres intersecting a wall are clipped by the domain.

Grid and initial velocity/temperature settings are disabled while playing.
Changing them while paused rebuilds on the next Step/Play. Reset always recreates
initial density and heat at the current source location, even with continuous
emission disabled. Timestep edits preserve the fields but trigger a cold pressure
solve. Display settings do not change the simulation.

## Implementation

`physics/interaction.py` defines the live-field allowlist and reset signature.
`DenseProjectedSmoke.update_settings` validates that initial conditions did not
change before updating immutable settings on the solver and projector. It does
not allocate textures, run compute passes, reset counters, or discard pressure.
New settings are consumed by the existing kernels at the next step.

The Blender runtime distinguishes live edits from grid/initial-state changes.
Live edits during its main-thread timer preserve solver ownership and simulation
time. External initial-state changes during playback stop the timer with a
Reset message. Pressure diagnostics continue to describe the last completed
step and are cleared when a new step starts.

The UI uses native collapsible sections for setup, source, and flow. Numerical
code remains independent of UI and Blender scene properties.

## Limits and next stage

One spherical emitter in a fixed box; no mouse brush, mesh emitter, collider,
volume rendering, or timeline cache. Live position edits emit at the next sampled
position, without interpolating a swept source path. Small sources may be poorly
resolved on coarse grids. Pressure is approximate, transport is diffusive and
nonconservative, and timestep remains fixed. No FPS guarantee is claimed.
Memory is unchanged from P0.5 (18.156 MiB at 64³, 144.625 MiB at 128³).
P0.7 is volume raymarching; P0.8 adds adaptive CFL.
