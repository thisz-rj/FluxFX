# P0.4 — temperature and buoyancy

Release **0.4.0**, based on P0.3 commit `50020e2`. The active Blender session now
uses `DenseThermalAdvection`; both prior backends remain intact for comparison.

## Thermal model and units

Two cell-centered R32F textures hold **temperature excess above ambient**, in
kelvin. Ambient excess is zero. Signed values are intentional: positive is hot,
negative is cold. This is a transported scalar, not a combustion or energy solver.

At each W face, average the two adjacent cell densities and temperature excesses:

`a_z = thermal_lift * theta_face - density_weight * rho_face`

Then `W_next = W_advected + dt * a_z`. Positive is Blender's +Z direction. The
thermal coefficient has units m/s²/K; density weight has units m/s² per unit of
the prototype's density. They include gravity scaling, so no additional gravity
factor is applied. At the top/bottom face, reuse the nearest interior scalar
(one-sided constant extension). U/V receive no direct buoyancy force.

Temperature transport reuses the signed scalar advection kernel. Cooling is
`theta *= exp(-cooling * dt)`, which relaxes both hot and cold values toward
ambient. A signed K/s source is then added with the existing compact sphere mask.
Default initial heat is +100 K, continuous heat source +100 K/s, cooling 0.5/s,
thermal lift 0.005 m/s²/K, density weight 0.05 m/s² per density unit. Initial
translation and rotation default to zero so buoyancy drives the new demo.

## Pass order and resources

1. Self-advect U/V/W from the same old velocity state.
2. Apply buoyancy to the advected W using the **previous scalar state**.
3. Advect density using the forced velocity; apply decay and density source.
4. Advect temperature using that same forced velocity; apply cooling and heat source.
5. Commit all front/back references together.

New heat affects acceleration on the next step. This is a deliberate explicit
splitting choice. Initial temperature is seeded on Reset, so initial buoyancy
can act on the first step. `reset(seed=False)` clears density and temperature,
while reinitializing velocity from its initial-flow settings.

There are 11 field textures: two density, two temperature, six velocity, and one
W scratch target. Scratch is rotated through the W roles to avoid both in-place
reads/writes and allocating per step. Field storage is **11,649,024 bytes** at
64³ and **92,733,440 bytes** at 128³, excluding driver/shader/display overhead.
Normal playback has no CPU voxel loops or full-field readbacks. Teardown drops
all thermal and inherited resources after removing timers and draw handlers.

## Validation — M5 Pro, Metal, Blender 5.3 b2e052b7172a

- **32 standalone tests pass**, including hot/cold/density sign, force equilibrium,
  vertical-only acceleration, face interpolation, boundary extension, signed
  cooling, signed heat-source rate, parameter validation, and no-force equivalence.
- Three four-step GPU/CPU coupled tests pass on a non-cubic physical domain:
  signed temperature, cooling-only, and negative heat injection.
- Maximum errors: density **1.914e-6**, temperature **9.556e-5 K**, velocity
  **3.102e-8 m/s**. Tolerances: density/velocity 2e-5, temperature 5e-4.
- Exact uniform-force tests pass: hot rises, cold sinks, density sinks, and
  balanced hot/dense material has zero acceleration. U/V remain zero.
- With thermal lift and density weight zero, GPU density and velocity are **exactly
  equal** to P0.3 after four steps, including nonzero initial rotation/translation.
- Seeded temperature resets exactly; empty resets clear both scalars.
- 64³ and 128³ each complete 30 steps with finite fields and nonnegative density.
  Maximum upward speeds after one simulated second are about 0.352 and 0.375 m/s;
  maximum temperature excesses are about 89.15 and 92.53 K. These are specific
  test observations, not performance or resolution-independence guarantees.
- Nine playback/resource lifecycle checks pass. Undo/load cleanup is tested by
  direct callback invocation. Both density and orange temperature slice views
  were inspected in the Blender viewport.

## What still limits the result

There is no pressure projection, incompressibility constraint, solid wall,
collision field, combustion, adaptive CFL controller, or volume renderer.
The current compressible transport can retain a compact source-shaped blob and
cannot establish realistic smoke plume motion. The next milestone is **P0.5**:
MAC divergence, weighted-Jacobi pressure solve, pressure-gradient subtraction,
and numerical measurement of divergence before/after projection.
